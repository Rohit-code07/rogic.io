When you log in to the Service using Google OAuth, we collect the following information:

  • Required items: Email address, name/username, profile image URL, and Google unique identifier (Subject ID).

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as your account remains active. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us via email. Upon request, all your stored user records and puzzle history will be permanently deleted from our database.
