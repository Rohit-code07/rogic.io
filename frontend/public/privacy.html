Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how rogic.io ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personal account access, statistics, and history tracking, we allow authentication using Google OAuth. When you log in, we request access to the following information via AWS Cognito:

  • Google Account Identifier (Sub ID): A unique key to identify your login.
  • Email Address: Used to map your account uniquely and secure your user data.
  • Profile Picture URL: Displayed in the header and My Page as your profile avatar.
  • Name / Username: Displayed as your default display name on the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and verify identity.
  • To save and display your completed nonogram puzzles history and achievements.
  • To compile public ranking leaderboards.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties under any circumstances, except when required by law or as necessary to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your database record, including history and cleared records, will be kept for as long as your account remains active. If you wish to delete your account or requests deletion of your personal data, please contact the administrator.

5. Security

We apply standard security protocols to protect user data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no database transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
